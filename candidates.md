# Candidates

## Winner (189 characters)

Checked in for two days, stayed for the eclipses. Pros: breathable air, dramatic tides, cheese. Cons: the apex species pays rent to live on its own planet. Overall 8/10, would abduct again.

## Runner-up (197 characters)

Would return. The gravity is gentle, the sunsets are subsidised by pollution but still gorgeous, and the dominant species apologises to furniture it bumps into. Book the oceans before they warm up.

## Why the winner won

Twelve candidates were drafted and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria, in the judge's order: reads like a real review first, then funniness, originality, and tightness. Three drafts (204, 205 and 215 characters) fell at the gate for breaking the 200-character limit. The winner is the most review-shaped line in the pool — check-in, Pros/Cons, an overall score and a would-return verdict — and every joke in it (rent on its own planet, "would abduct again") is one only an alien tourist could make, with no wasted words in 189 characters. The runner-up is charming and concrete, but it carries no score and its middle sags, so it lost the final.
